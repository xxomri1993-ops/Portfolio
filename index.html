How it gets made

One operator, one pipeline, four stages — from a brief to a file that's ready to air.

  1. 01

    Concept

    Brief to script to storyboard. The idea is locked and shot-listed before a single frame is generated.

  2. 02

    Generation

    Characters, environments and coverage produced with generative video and image models, held to one visual language.

  3. 03

    Edit & Sound

    Cut for pacing and retention, then graded, scored, and finished with voiceover and sound design.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    Broadcast-ready masters plus every aspect ratio and cutdown the campaign needs.
